# Painted Studio assets
New art is original AI-generated hand-painted tabletop-style artwork, processed from the local atlases in asset-source/studio. Thirty board paintings, thirty main sprites, thirteen prop sprites and thirty composited cards are supplied. Transparent sprites preserve enclosed highlights and remove exterior white backgrounds. The sun has its own source illustration.

Variants combine different existing materials with mechanic/layout/rule changes; they are not 110 unique illustrations. Original ten-game art and music are preserved. New music is procedural, locally generated instrumental audio, not recorded orchestral music. Twelve effects are reused thematically, with playback-rate variation. The library does not contain a distinct recorded effect for every individual object.

Runtime assets are all local. Review applicable rights, distribution licenses and store disclosures before commercial release. No external texture service or music API is needed to play.
